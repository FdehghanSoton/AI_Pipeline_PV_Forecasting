After regular hourly reindexing, 868 PV measurements were absent, corresponding
to $9.25\%$ of the hourly grid. Missingness was recorded before numerical
replacement; the zero value used in tensor construction is therefore a finite
placeholder rather than an observed zero-generation label. The CNN receives a
separate historical PV-availability channel, enabling it to distinguish these
placeholders from genuine zero output. Its nine input channels comprise
normalised historical PV, a past/target-day indicator, the PV-availability
mask, and six weather variables. For each target day, the PV channel contains
only the preceding seven days; the target-day PV block is zeroed. Moreover,
outer-validation and test-day PV values are masked wherever those days would
otherwise occur in another sample's history window. During optimisation,
missing target hours receive zero loss weight (valid night-time and daylight
hours receive weights of one and four, respectively), and all reported metrics
exclude missing targets. Train--validation partitions are defined on complete
calendar days so that a day cannot contribute targets to both subsets.
